Types of Entities

1. Strong Entity
A Strong Entity is an entity that can exist independently of other entities. It has attributes that can uniquely identify it (like a primary key).

Think of it like this:

A teacher in a school can exist independently - they have their own unique employee ID, regardless of what classes they teach or students they have.
Example: In a School System
Teachers (identified by TeacherID)
Students (identified by StudentID)
Departments (identified by DepartmentCode)


2. Weak Entity
A Weak Entity is an entity that cannot exist independently and must be associated with another strong entity. It typically does not have a unique identifier of its own and relies on a foreign key to identify it.

Think of it like this:

A room number in a building only makes sense when associated with that specific building - Room 101 could exist in multiple buildings, but "Room 101 in Building A" is unique.
Example: In a School System
Course Sections (depends on Course)
Student Assignments (depends on Course and Student)
Class Schedule Slots (depends on Room and Time Period)
